Outreach Drafter

An AI employee that researches a prospect and drafts outreach for Zamp's sales team

What this does

You give it a person's name and company. Like a new sales hire, it looks them up online, keeps only facts it can prove, and writes a short first email. Then it stops and waits for you. Nothing is ever sent on its own.
